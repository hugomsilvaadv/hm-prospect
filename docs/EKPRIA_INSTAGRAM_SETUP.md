# EKPRIA — Instagram launch checklist

**Brand:** EKPRIA — outsourced B2B receivables management and administrative collection services.

## Operator checklist

1. Official Instagram profile confirmed by the operator: **[@ekpria](https://www.instagram.com/ekpria/)**. Keep this handle in business configuration.
2. Set professional account category to **Business / Serviços empresariais** or the closest available.
3. Set profile display name to **EKPRIA | Gestão de Recebíveis**.
4. Upload the approved brand photo optimized for circular profile crop.
5. Set a clear bio, without invented performance claims:

   💼 Gestão terceirizada de cobranças B2B
   📊 Recebíveis, negociação e inadimplência
   🤝 Soluções administrativas para empresas
   👇 Fale com a EKPRIA

6. Add a business contact destination *only after the real number/site is confirmed*. Never hard-code an unverified WhatsApp or domain.
7. Activate professional account security (2FA), designate the person who will operate the account.
8. Prepare and pin three introductory posts: **O que é a EKPRIA**, **O que podemos assumir da cobrança administrativa**, **Como funciona a contratação**.
9. Highlight suggested topics: **Serviços**, **Como funciona**, **Perguntas frequentes**, **Contato**.
10. Review business object, relevant CNAE, privacy notice, data processing agreements, collection procedures, and lawful outreach channels before offering operational collection services.
11. For Meta API integration, use the official app/business setup and proper access permissions; never publish Meta tokens or Instagram session cookies.
12. Live outreach remains **off** until a limited approved test, documented platform permissions and opt-out protections are verified.
